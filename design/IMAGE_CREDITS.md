# Demo covers

The demo projects' covers are logos drawn by `design/make_demo_logos.swift` (output in `design/demo-logos/`), no third-party images.

# Partner logos

Shown unmodified on the integration screens to say which service a connection talks to. They're trademarks of their owners and are used under each company's brand guidelines, not licensed with this repo.

| Asset | Source |
|---|---|
| Logo-revenuecat | RevenueCat app icon from the [RevenueCat press kit](https://www.revenuecat.com/press-and-brand-resources/) |
| Logo-youtube | YouTube icon (`favicon_192x192.png`) from YouTube's branding assets on gstatic.com, per the [YouTube API Services branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/youtube/branding): unmodified colors on a solid white tile, linking to the connected channel |
| Logo-bluesky | White butterfly from the [Bluesky brand assets](https://bsky.social/about/support/icons), on Bluesky blue; links to the connected profile ([trademark policy](https://bsky.social/about/support/trademarks) §4.1) |
| Logo-tiktok | `TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png` from the logo pack on [TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/getting-started-design-guidelines), scaled to 360 px; shown for the TikTok sign-in integration |
| Logo-instagram | Gradient glyph (`Instagram_Glyph_Gradient.png`) from the [Instagram brand resources](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/), scaled to 360 px, unmodified and full size, white inside its outline (Meta allows the glyph in a lineup of social icons) |
| Logo-shopify | Shopify bag glyph (`shopify_glyph.png`) from Shopify's brand assets, scaled to 360 px, unmodified on a white tile |
| Logo-stripe | White Stripe wordmark from the [Stripe logo kit](https://stripe.com/newsroom/brand-assets), on Stripe's purple as its guidelines allow for dark backgrounds |

App Store Connect keeps a generic symbol: Apple doesn't allow its logos or icons in third-party apps without a license. The other services keep generic symbols: Vercel, npm and PostHog require permission for their logos, and the rest publish no clear rule. The original brand downloads stay out of the repo (`design/01 Static Glyph/`, `design/Dev Portal Logo Pack/`, `design/02 - Glyph/`, `design/Download-on-the-App-Store/` are ignored).
