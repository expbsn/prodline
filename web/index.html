How it works

Short bursts.
Real progress.

Prodline turns the big vague thing into two-week stretches with a few checkpoints, keeps you showing up every day, and brings the numbers to you.

01

Plan it

A handful of checkpoints, each with a few goals. Due on the days you actually work.

02

Show up

Tick one goal a day. Commits count by themselves, and the streak keeps you honest.

03

Watch it land

Revenue, visits and downloads show up on their own, per day and per project.
