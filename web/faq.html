FAQ & support

Questions, answered.

Can't find yours? Write to kianpal731@gmail.com and you'll hear back from a human, usually within two days.

The app

What is Prodline?

An iPhone app for side projects. You plan each project in short build phases with a few checkpoints, tick goals as you go, keep a daily streak, and watch the numbers that matter (revenue, visits, downloads) come in on their own. At the end you decide: keep it, pivot it, or let it go.

What does it cost?

Prodline is free during the beta.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Widgets work on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and deadline Live Activities appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

How do I join the beta?

Leave your email on the home page. We let testers in batches and send a TestFlight invite when your spot opens.

Does it have dark mode?

Yes. It follows your iPhone by default; Me → Configuration → Appearance keeps it light or dark.

Numbers and integrations

Which services can I connect?

Stripe, App Store Connect, RevenueCat, YouTube, Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad, Shopify, Paddle, Polar, Plausible, PostHog, Fathom Analytics, Simple Analytics, Umami, Vercel, Netlify, Kit, Buttondown, beehiiv, npm, Hacker News, Product Hunt and Bluesky. GitHub links your plan and commits, and anything else can report through your own endpoint.

Are my API keys safe?

Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device (and in your iCloud Keychain if you use it). The app sends each key only to the service it belongs to, directly from your iPhone. Prodline has no server that sees your keys or your numbers.

How often do numbers update?

Every 30 seconds by default while the app is open (Me → Configuration → Live refresh), and in the background when iOS allows it.

Can I connect something that isn't listed?

Yes: point a project at your own endpoint that returns a small JSON document with your metrics. The format is described in the app under the project's Connection → Your own endpoint.

GitHub and instant updates

What does the GitHub link do?

Prodline reads your repository's commits, issues and an optional prodline.json file with your checkpoints and goals. Goals in that file tick themselves when you mark them done in a commit.

What are instant updates?

Optional. A GitHub webhook goes to Prodline's relay, which sends a silent push to your iPhone so the app syncs within seconds of a push. Without it, the app checks on its own every few minutes. You can turn it off under Me → Configuration (type off as the relay address).

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and in your own private iCloud database, so it syncs across your devices. There's no Prodline account. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Me → Delete all data removes every project, goal, metric and stored key. Deleting the app removes what's left on the device; iCloud data can be removed under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. To remove relay or waitlist entries, write to kianpal731@gmail.com.

Does Prodline track me?

No. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.
